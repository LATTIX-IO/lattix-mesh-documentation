Compliance outcomes in SaaS are shared between Lattix and each customer tenant.

## Responsibility Model

Lattix is generally responsible for:

- Operating the managed SaaS platform securely
- Maintaining platform-level security and availability controls
- Providing customer-visible security/compliance artifacts as applicable

Customer tenants are generally responsible for:

- Correct role assignment and access governance
- Policy design, approvals, and operational enforcement decisions
- Data classification, handling rules, and retention configuration
- Internal audit preparation and control evidence assembly

## Why Shared Responsibility Matters

Even with strong platform controls, tenant-side misconfiguration can create compliance gaps. Governance processes are as important as technical controls.

## Customer Operating Practices

- Define policy owners and approvers
- Review role assignments regularly
- Test high-risk policy changes before broad rollout
- Keep evidence trails for governance and audits

## Scope Clarification

This page describes common responsibility boundaries for SaaS use. Confirm exact obligations with your legal, compliance, and procurement teams.
