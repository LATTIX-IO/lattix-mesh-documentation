Use this guidance to map Lattix capabilities to your required compliance frameworks.

## Common Mapping Process

1. List framework controls relevant to your scope.
2. Identify Lattix features that support each objective.
3. Identify tenant-operated procedures needed to complete control coverage.
4. Define evidence sources and retention expectations.

## Example Mapping Categories

- Access control and least privilege
- Identity and authentication assurance
- Policy governance and change management
- Monitoring, logging, and incident handling
- Evidence retention and audit traceability

## Mapping Best Practices

- Map to control objectives, not tool names
- Avoid one-to-many ambiguity without clear ownership
- Record assumptions and boundary conditions
- Review mappings after major process or policy changes

## Important Note

Framework mapping does not automatically equal certification. Your organization remains responsible for validating implementation and audit readiness.
