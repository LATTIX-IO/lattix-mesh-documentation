## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does using Lattix automatically make us compliant?

No. Lattix provides capabilities that support compliance objectives, but your organization remains responsible for control operation, governance, and audit readiness.

### Who owns compliance in our tenant?

Compliance ownership is shared across tenant admin, security, and business stakeholders. Define clear owners for each control domain.

### What should we provide to auditors?

Provide scoped evidence tied to control objectives, including policy governance artifacts, access decision history, and remediation records.

### How often should we review controls?

Set a regular cadence (for example weekly operational checks and quarterly governance reviews), based on your risk profile and obligations.

## Audit Prep Checklist

- [ ] Applicable frameworks and scope documented
- [ ] Shared responsibility model documented internally
- [ ] Control owners and RACI confirmed
- [ ] Role and access review evidence available
- [ ] Policy change and approval records available
- [ ] Security review and incident records available
- [ ] Evidence redaction and packaging procedure tested
- [ ] Pre-audit dry run completed

## Final Readiness Gate

Before audit submission, verify that every required control has:

- an owner
- operating evidence
- a clear narrative of how the control is implemented and monitored
