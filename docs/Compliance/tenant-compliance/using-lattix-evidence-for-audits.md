This guide explains how to collect and present Lattix-generated evidence for internal and external audits.

## Evidence Types to Prepare

- Policy change records and approvals
- Access decision outcomes for in-scope workflows
- Role assignment/review artifacts
- Investigation and remediation records

## Audit Preparation Workflow

1. Define evidence request scope and period
2. Collect relevant records from approved sources
3. Validate completeness and timestamp integrity
4. Redact sensitive information not required for auditors
5. Package evidence with clear control mapping notes

## Evidence Quality Checklist

- Attributable (who/what/when)
- Traceable (linked to policy and workflow context)
- Consistent (matches control objective wording)
- Reviewable (readable by non-operators)

## Security and Privacy Considerations

- Share minimum necessary data
- Follow internal redaction and handling policies
- Do not include secrets or unnecessary personal data
