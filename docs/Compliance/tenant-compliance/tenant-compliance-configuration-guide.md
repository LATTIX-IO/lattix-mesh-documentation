Use this guide to configure tenant operations in a compliance-friendly way.

## Step 1: Define Compliance Scope

- Identify applicable frameworks and internal standards
- Define in-scope data types and workflows
- Assign control owners (admin, security, business)

## Step 2: Configure Access Governance

- Enforce least-privilege role assignments
- Require approvals for high-impact policy changes
- Establish review cadence for role and policy drift

## Step 3: Configure Policy Governance

- Use deny-by-default baseline
- Define exception rules with owner and expiration
- Test policy behavior for expected allow/deny paths

## Step 4: Configure Evidence Workflow

- Define which logs/outcomes are needed for audits
- Set retention practices aligned to policy obligations
- Define who can access and export evidence

## Step 5: Operationalize Reviews

- Weekly or monthly security/compliance review routine
- Quarterly access and policy governance review
- Document corrective actions and closure evidence

## Validation Checklist

- Control owners assigned
- Review cadence scheduled
- Policy change workflow documented
- Evidence process tested end-to-end
