Attributes evolve as org structures and business processes change. Treat changes as security-impacting events.

## Lifecycle Stages

- Define
- Validate
- Activate
- Monitor
- Retire

## Change Management Controls

- Require impact review before changing critical attributes
- Test policy outcomes for changed values and null cases
- Communicate downstream effects to policy owners
- Track versioned mapping updates

## Operational Pattern

Maintain an attribute catalog with owner, source, quality requirements, and dependent policy sets.
