Auditability in a zero trust model means you can reconstruct key decisions and actions with enough context to support security and compliance needs.

## Evidence Types

- Policy decision outcomes
- User and role activity records
- Governance actions (approvals, policy changes)
- Connector and identity context signals

## Evidence Quality Criteria

- Accurate and time-ordered
- Attributable to actor and policy context
- Retained according to compliance obligations
- Accessible to authorized reviewers

## Governance Operating Model

- Assign clear owners for policy, identity, and review
- Define regular review cadence
- Track exceptions and their expiration
- Require post-change verification for high-risk policy updates

## Compliance Readiness Outcome

When governance is healthy, teams can answer:

- Who accessed what and why?
- Which policy allowed or denied it?
- What changed, when, and by whom?
- How is least privilege maintained over time?
