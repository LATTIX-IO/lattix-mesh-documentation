A stable governance cadence prevents policy drift and access creep.

## Suggested Cadence

- Weekly: review high-risk outcomes and exceptions
- Monthly: review policy effectiveness and attribute quality
- Quarterly: role model and access scope review

## RACI Pattern Example

- Responsible: policy domain owner
- Accountable: tenant security lead
- Consulted: business/process owner
- Informed: affected user groups and operations teams

## Metrics That Matter

- Exception count and age
- Deny/allow trend shifts after policy changes
- Time to resolve high-risk decision investigations
