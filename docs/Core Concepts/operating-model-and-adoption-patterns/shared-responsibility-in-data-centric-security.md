Data-centric security requires coordination across admin, security, and business teams.

## Responsibility Domains

- Tenant Admin: policy lifecycle, role governance, connector oversight
- Security User: monitoring, investigation, control validation
- Business Owner: policy intent, approval context, exception justification

## Why Shared Responsibility Fails

- Unclear policy ownership
- No decision on who approves exceptions
- Missing feedback loop from incidents to policy updates

## Minimal Responsibility Model

- Name one owner per policy domain
- Define approval thresholds for high-risk changes
- Review outcomes and exceptions on a fixed cadence
