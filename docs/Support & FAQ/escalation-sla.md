Use this page to define and follow your support escalation workflow.

## Escalation Workflow

1. Open support request with severity and impact details
2. Provide required troubleshooting context (redacted)
3. Track assigned owner and next update time
4. Escalate if impact or risk increases

## Suggested Severity Model

- **Critical:** Security risk or widespread production outage
- **High:** Major function degraded for key users
- **Medium:** Partial impact with workaround
- **Low:** Non-blocking question or minor issue

## Suggested Response Targets

Set targets in your customer agreement and internal runbooks. Example model:

- Critical: fastest response path with active updates
- High: same business day response
- Medium: next business day response
- Low: standard support queue

## Escalation Triggers

- No progress against agreed update cadence
- Issue severity increases
- New security/compliance impact identified

## Communication Standards

- Keep incident timeline current
- Communicate impact and mitigation status clearly
- Avoid sharing secrets/sensitive artifacts in updates
