Use this page when you need help with platform configuration, access behavior, or operational issues.

## Before Contacting Support

Collect:

- Tenant/workspace identifier
- Time window when issue occurred
- User role affected (not credentials)
- Clear summary of expected vs actual behavior
- Any relevant redacted screenshots or logs

## Severity Guidance (Suggested)

- **Critical:** Major production impact or security concern
- **High:** Significant workflow disruption
- **Medium:** Limited scope issue or workaround available
- **Low:** Documentation/question/request

## Safe Support Practices

Do **not** send:

- Passwords, tokens, keys, secrets
- Full sensitive data payloads
- Unredacted personal information unless explicitly required and approved

## Include in Your Request

- Business impact summary
- Steps to reproduce
- Recent changes (policy/connector/access) that may be related

## Escalation

See **Escalation & SLA** for response expectations and escalation workflow.
