Before onboarding your team, confirm the following prerequisites.

## Organizational Prerequisites

- An active Lattix tenant/workspace provisioned by your onboarding contact
- A designated **Tenant Admin** to own initial setup and governance
- Approval for initial user role assignments (admin, security, end-user roles)

## Identity and Access Prerequisites

- Your organization identity provider and ICAM contacts identified
- Required identity attributes available for ABAC policy design
- Security review completed for SSO and role-mapping decisions

## Data Governance Prerequisites

- Initial data classification approach defined
- High-level access policies and exceptions documented
- Named owners for policy review and approval

## Operational Prerequisites

- A small pilot user group (recommended) for first rollout
- Defined support path for user onboarding issues
- Agreement on change-control and policy promotion process

## Do Not Share in Documentation or Tickets

For security hygiene, never include:

- Passwords, tokens, keys, certificates, secrets
- Private tenant identifiers not required for support
- Internal network details not requested by authorized support staff

## Ready Check

You are ready to continue when:

- Tenant Admin is assigned
- Identity and ICAM contacts are confirmed
- Pilot group is identified
- Governance owners are named
